Website Evaluations (CRAP Principles)

Google Homepage

  • Contrast: Excellent black-on-white contrast. Search button pops with blue background.
  • Repetition: Roboto font, rounded buttons, and spacing used consistently everywhere.
  • Alignment: Logo, search bar, buttons perfectly centered and aligned on invisible grid.
  • Proximity: Logo + search + buttons grouped as one unit; footer links grouped by category.

Arngren (Terrible Electronics Site)

  • Contrast: Poor - rainbow colors clash, tiny fonts hard to read on busy backgrounds.
  • Repetition: None - fonts, colors, button styles change randomly everywhere.
  • Alignment: Terrible - products scattered randomly, no grid alignment.
  • Proximity: Confusing - unrelated items jammed together, no logical grouping.
