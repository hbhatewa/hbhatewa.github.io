🦇 Our Story

Every café has a beginning. Ours started underground.

How the Cave Came to Be

The Bat Cave Café was founded in 2026 by Harsh, who had grown tired of sterile, over-lit coffee shops that felt more like waiting rooms than places to actually sit and think.

He found an old basement space in the heart of the city — low ceilings, exposed stone walls, a single narrow staircase leading down from the street. Most people saw a problem. Harsh saw a cave.

"I didn't want people to feel like they were grabbing a coffee. I wanted them to feel like they were disappearing into somewhere." — Harsh, founder

Over three months he renovated by hand — salvaging timber, hanging Edison bulbs from the stone ceiling, and building the bar from reclaimed wood. The cave was ready before he even had a name for it.

Why the Bats?

The bat theme wasn't planned. On the first night Harsh stayed late finishing the renovation, he noticed a small colony of bats roosting in the far corner of the basement ceiling. Instead of calling pest control, he left them alone.

Bats, he argued, are the most misunderstood creatures — nocturnal, quiet, essential to their ecosystem, and almost always found in the most interesting places. That sounded exactly like the kind of café he wanted to run.

"The bats were here first. I just built a café around them." — Harsh, founder

The colony has since moved on, but their spirit stayed. Every drink name, every amber light, every dark roast blend is a small nod to that first night in the cave.
