Espresso Fundamentals

Every drink at the Bat Cave Café starts with a well-pulled espresso. Before touching any other preparation, a barista must master the shot.

Espresso is made by forcing hot water at high pressure through finely ground coffee, producing a concentrated shot with a thick reddish-brown crema on top.

Dose: 18g Yield: 36g Ratio: 1:2 Time: 28–32 seconds Temperature: 93°C Pressure: 9 bars
  • Grind finer if the shot runs under 25 seconds
  • Grind coarser if the shot runs over 35 seconds
  • Always purge the group head 2–3 seconds before locking in the portafilter
Milk Steaming and Texture

At the Bat Cave Café we aim for silky microfoam — milk that pours like wet paint and integrates seamlessly with espresso.

Always start with cold milk straight from the fridge. Submerge the steam wand just below the surface and angle the pitcher slightly. You should hear a gentle hiss, not a loud scream.

Start temp: 4–6°C Final temp: 60–65°C Flat White → 2–3mm foam Latte → 5–8mm foam Cappuccino → 1–2cm foam
  • Never re-steam milk — discard and start fresh
  • Purge and wipe the steam wand after every use
Signature Recipes

Every barista must memorise these house recipes before their first solo shift. Do not adjust ratios without manager approval.

The Lunar Latte and Eclipse Mocha are the two most ordered drinks in the cave — consistency on these is non-negotiable.

-- Lunar Latte -- Espresso: 36g double shot Milk: 160ml, medium foam Vanilla syrup: 10ml (2 pumps) Top: pinch of silver dust -- Eclipse Mocha -- Espresso: 36g double shot Dark choc sauce: 15ml Milk: 150ml, light foam Top: dark cocoa powder
  • Add chocolate sauce before espresso on the mocha — not after
  • Silver dust is in the spice drawer, not on the bar
Cold Brew

Cold brew is made in-house every 20 hours using a slow steep method. It is never made by pouring hot espresso over ice — that is an iced americano and is a separate item.

The opening barista starts a new batch each morning for the following day's service. Every batch must be labelled with brew start time and ready time.

Coffee (coarse grind): 120g Cold filtered water: 1000ml Steep time: 18–20 hours Temperature: 4°C (fridge) Serve ratio: 1:1 brew to water or milk
  • Use coarse grind only — fine grind over-extracts the cold brew
  • Never steep beyond 22 hours
  • Cold brew keeps for up to 7 days refrigerated
Equipment and Maintenance

Every barista is responsible for equipment cleanliness. A clean machine pulls better shots and lasts longer. Do not leave the bar without completing the end-of-shift checklist.

The espresso machine must be backflushed with cleaner tablets every closing shift. Skipping this damages the group head seals over time.

Every shot: purge group head, knock puck immediately Hourly: wipe wand, drip tray, counter End of shift: backflush (3x plain + 1x cleaner) soak portafilters 20 min empty and rinse drip trays wipe machine exterior
  • Report unusual machine sounds to the manager immediately
  • Log all grinder usage — burrs replaced every 300kg
  • Steam wand o-rings replaced monthly
