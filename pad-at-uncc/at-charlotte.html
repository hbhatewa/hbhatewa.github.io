Phi Alpha Delta at Charlotte

The path to law school can feel overwhelming, especially when students are trying to figure out applications, professional expectations, and career goals on their own.

At UNC Charlotte, Phi Alpha Delta creates a home for pre-law students who want guidance, community, and meaningful opportunities as they prepare for the future.

Join Our Chapter

Built for Niners

Freshmen exploring the legal field can find direction early.

Seniors preparing applications can find support and connection.

Students of all majors can build community through a shared interest in law.

Open Membership, Real Opportunity

Phi Alpha Delta at Charlotte believes students should not have to compete for the chance to grow professionally. If you are interested in the law and in good standing with the University, there is a place for you here.

There is no cut process and no exclusive rush model. We focus on ambition, commitment, and creating access for students who want to explore the legal profession.

What Members Gain

01

LSAT Support

Members can access valuable law-school-prep resources and discounts from major LSAT prep providers, helping make preparation more affordable and practical.

02

A Larger Network

Joining PAD means becoming part of a wide professional network that includes alumni, attorneys, judges, and legal professionals across many stages of the field.

03

Professional Confidence

Through chapter programming and campus involvement, members build the confidence and experience needed to take the next step toward law school and legal careers.

The Charlotte Experience

Guest Speakers

Hear directly from judges, attorneys, and representatives from the DA’s office about legal careers, courtroom realities, and professional pathways.

Law School Tours

Visit regional law schools and get a closer look at the environments, expectations, and opportunities that may shape your future.

Peer Community

Build connections with other UNC Charlotte students who understand the challenges of the pre-law path and want to support one another.

Leadership Growth

Take part in a chapter culture that encourages initiative, involvement, and meaningful leadership experience beyond the classroom.

Take the Next Step

Whether you are just exploring the legal field or preparing applications now, PAD at Charlotte gives you a place to learn, connect, and move forward with confidence.
