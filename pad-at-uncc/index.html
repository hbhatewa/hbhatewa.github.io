Welcome to Phi Alpha Delta Law Fraternity

Phi Alpha Delta is the largest co-ed law fraternity in the United States, founded in 1902. Our UNC Charlotte chapter connects pre-law students, law students, and legal professionals through networking, professional development, community service, and lifelong connections.

Whether you're exploring a legal career, seeking professional connections, or looking to give back to the Charlotte community, PAD offers opportunities for growth and impact.

Why Join Our Chapter?

What to Expect as a New Member

New members can expect a welcoming environment where they can learn more about legal careers, meet other students with similar interests, and begin building professional confidence.

Throughout the semester, members may attend chapter meetings, guest speaker events, service opportunities, and programs related to law school preparation and professional development.

Getting involved does not require prior legal experience — just an interest in learning, participating, and connecting with the chapter community.
