About Phi Alpha Delta

Phi Alpha Delta Law Fraternity, International is a professional legal organization that supports students interested in the law through education, leadership, service, and professional connection.

At UNC Charlotte, our chapter helps students explore legal careers, prepare for law school, and become part of a supportive pre-law community focused on growth and impact.

Who We Are

Phi Alpha Delta’s pre-law program is designed to help undergraduate students make informed decisions about pursuing a legal career, selecting law schools, and preparing for law school.

Our UNC Charlotte chapter brings that mission to campus by creating opportunities for students to learn from professionals, engage with fellow members, and develop the academic and leadership skills that matter in the legal field.

We want members to leave our chapter more informed, more connected, and more confident about their future path.

What Defines Our Chapter

Professionalism: Building habits and connections that support future legal success.

Community: Creating a space where pre-law students can grow together.

Service: Giving back while learning the responsibilities of legal leadership.

Opportunity: Opening doors to events, mentorship, and meaningful experiences.

Phi Alpha Delta at UNC Charlotte helps students explore legal careers, connect with others who share similar goals, and grow through service, leadership, and professional development.

Our Mission

Phi Alpha Delta exists to support students who are interested in the law by promoting academic growth, professional development, service, and leadership. Our chapter creates a welcoming environment where members can explore the legal field and learn more about future opportunities.

We aim to help students feel more informed, more prepared, and more connected as they consider careers in law and related professions.

Member Benefits

Members gain access to networking opportunities, chapter programming, leadership experience, and resources related to legal careers and law school. PAD also gives students a chance to build meaningful relationships with peers, alumni, and professionals in the legal community.

  • Professional networking and guest speakers
  • Leadership and service opportunities
  • Community with other pre-law students
  • Exposure to law school and legal career paths

Ways to Get Involved

Students can become involved by attending chapter meetings, participating in campus events, volunteering in service activities, and taking part in programs designed to strengthen their academic and professional goals.

Members who want to take a larger role can also pursue leadership positions and help shape the direction of the chapter through planning and outreach.
